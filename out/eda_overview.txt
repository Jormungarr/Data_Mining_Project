8  Correlation with ILI_PMP, under two different aggregations
   The two panels answer different questions and give different numbers.

   response                   pooled    weekly mean
   ae_first_attend            +0.355         +0.695
   ae_to_med_ip               +0.158         +0.635
   med_bed_occ_rate           +0.351         +0.571
   ped_bed_occ_rate           +0.568         +0.697

   pooled      = all 2,448 hospital-weeks together. Mixes 'is this hospital
                 large?' into the correlation, which dilutes it.
   weekly mean = one value per week, 144 points. This is the more meaningful
                 of the two, and matches DATA_GUIDE Section 6.

   Note: DATA_GUIDE quotes the same correlations from hk_weekly.csv (the HA
   Overall aggregate row), which covers slightly more hospitals. The two
   agree exactly for ae_first_attend and differ by at most 0.005 elsewhere.
   Both are correct; they are just different aggregations.

   Both are descriptive associations, not evidence of causation.
